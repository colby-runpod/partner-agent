# Lead review request clarity

New reviews extract requested_partnership, requested_action and offered_value from the original submission. Render these before the suggested next step; distinguish the requested model from internal routing and label inferred models. Missing fields have explicit fallbacks, and old reviews are not silently reinterpreted. Both storage backends preserve the fields for subsequent thread actions.

Research instructions distinguish lack of verification from evidence of misrepresentation, require specific evidence URLs, and avoid treating a reply as a commercial commitment. This does not change deterministic qualification thresholds or re-score historical records. Existing reviews are unchanged until deliberately re-reviewed.

Daily recaps group leads by Needs your decision, Actioned / handed off, and Parked. Each lead has a separate linked company heading, requested partnership, request, score/assessment, recorded action status, and next step or parked context. Confirmed handoffs retain destination links. Older records fall back to their saved type and headline; no fresh research or reassessment is implied.

All leads in the reporting window are included. Recaps split at complete paragraph boundaries into numbered posts of approximately 3,500 characters. Receipts are persisted after each post. An uncertain or interrupted multipart send holds the window for reconciliation without automatically resending earlier parts. Coverage and schedule remain unchanged.
