# Program readiness and applicant response policy

Confirmed by Colby on 2026-10-28. Policy specification; not activated by the research-channel routing change.

## Program state is separate from lead quality

Maintain an explicit, dated program profile with owner, readiness, supported actions, prerequisites, exclusions and supporting Notion links. Research uses current approved program profiles, not a model's assumption that every partnership program is open. Unverified or stale program state requires an internal check, not an applicant questionnaire.

- Referral partners: actionable program path. A credible, genuine referral-partner request should immediately receive a specific next action: registration/onboarding, or review of a named customer referral against existing ownership and eligibility. No automatic agreement, commission or registration approval. Do not equate referral-partner interest with affiliate-program interest.
- Reseller: being built, outlined, defined and evaluated. Prioritize exceptions only with credible identity, demonstrated customer demand/distribution, delivery capability, material Runpod value and a concrete executable motion. Do not promise a reseller program, discounts, terms or provisioning capabilities. General interest without evidence goes to monitor/self-service rather than another information-request email.
- Other partnership types: default to selective review until the owner's current program profile confirms an actionable path. Do not infer every program has the same readiness or bar.
- Specialist requests: clearly classified research, event, supply and startup requests are owner handoffs, not proof of qualification or approval. Research logs in C0CCBS9NU2V without mentioning Zoe until her identity is configured. Colby remains cc.

## Decision fields

Keep Route, Program readiness, Opportunity score (1-5), Evidence confidence, Disposition and Next action separate. Referral interest can be actionable without automatically receiving 4/5. A 4/5 requires credible identity plus verified readiness and meaningful customer/distribution value. A named, relevant referral with an executable next step can qualify for that band; unsupported referral claims cannot.

Research identity, product/delivery readiness, actual demand/distribution, existing relationship and likely value before deciding. Attribute claims, cite evidence, state unknowns and unavailable tools. Warm internal forwards do not raise scores. Only ask applicants questions that cannot reasonably be researched and materially change a worthwhile decision.

## Deferred affiliate email workflow

Sender: partners@runpod.io. Gmail signature selection: partners@ (confirmed by Colby).

The Gmail/Claude task owns this workflow; AWS has no direct Gmail authorization. For a clearly classified affiliate inquiry, prepare the standard current-program pointer in the original thread, select the partners@runpod.io From identity, and insert the exact configured partners@ signature once. Preserve recipients, quoted context and attachments where relevant. Verify the actual saved draft's sender and body before sending, not just its intended configuration. A group address alone does not prove send-as access. Never silently fall back to Colby's personal sender or signature; hold on missing alias, missing signature or mismatched readback.

Do not infer that changing From through an API inserts the Gmail signature. The connected draft API supports From and body updates, but the configured send-as identity and actual signature still require verification in Gmail. Use the UI if required to select the configured signature. Retain one outbound receipt per source inquiry; do not resend on uncertain outcomes. Respect opt-outs and suppression. Distinguish affiliate/referral links from referral partnership, paid sponsorship, creator requests and technology integration.

Automatic sending is requested, but is not enabled in this release.
